FEATURES

Flat Speakers are the perfect blend of sound quality, simplicity and versatility

Balanced Response Across All Audible Frequencies

Regular speakers use many drivers to highlight frequencies. Flat Speakers provide consistent and balanced bass, mid, and high frequencies, with no need for additional equipment to enjoy high-quality sound

Bidirectional Speaker

Flat Speakers are ideal for live performers because they emit sound from both the front and back of the panel. This creates an almost 360-degree sound experience, eliminating the need for separate monitors

Minimum Room Interactions

Unlike bookshelf speakers, which can cause sound reflections and resonance within a room, Flat Speakers have minimal interaction with the room environment. Their design reduces unwanted reflections, providing a clearer sound experience

Light-Weight and Portable

With a compact form factor and weighing just 2 kg, Flat Speakers are perfect for on-the-go listening, providing high-quality sound without taking up too much space to transport

Sound Reach and Attenuation

Cone speakers often attenuate frequencies over distance, leading to different sound quality in different distances. Flat speakers do a better job at preserving the full range of frequencies over distance, ensuring consistent sound quality

Sound Clarity

Sound is naturally produced by vibrations that move air around an instrument. Flat Speakers replicate this process by creating vibration regions on the panel. This method closely mimics the original sound source

Sound Coverage

Cone speakers deliver the best sound when the listener is directly in front of them, often requiring multiple speakers to cover a room. Flat Speakers can project sound up to 170 degrees from a single panel, providing expansive coverage and easily filling the room with quality sound

No Mic Feedback

Flat Speakers are a great choice for live performances because they are inherently immune to feedback sounds caused by microphones or instruments. This ensures a smooth and uninterrupted audio experience
